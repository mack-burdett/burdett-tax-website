Who this is for

If one of these is you, we should talk.

Employed abroad

Salaried in another country, with a local tax return and a US one that still has to be filed.

Freelance and remote

Self-employed from anywhere. Self-employment tax survives the exclusion — that surprises almost everyone.

Business owners abroad

A local company you own makes you a shareholder of a controlled foreign corporation, with everything that follows.

Green card holders going home

Letting the card lapse does not end your US filing obligations, and leaving has its own rules.

Married to a non-US spouse

Filing status, whether to bring their income into the US net, and the ITIN question. The default is rarely the right answer.

Behind on filings

Several years unfiled, found out late, and not sure what happens next. This is more common than you think and there are routes back.
